05 / Delivery

Close control from
appraisal to completion.

Ēastmans takes a hands-on approach to development management. We coordinate the planning, design, professional and construction work required to move a project forward.

The objective is straightforward: maintain control of the detail while keeping the wider commercial outcome in view.

FROM APPRAISAL TO COMPLETION

01 SOURCEIdentify properties with credible development potential.
02 APPRAISEAssess planning, design, construction cost, programme, demand and commercial viability.
03 PLANDefine the development strategy and coordinate the appropriate professional team.
04 DELIVERManage the project closely through planning, construction and the decisions that arise along the way.
05 COMPLETEBring the property into its intended use with the development strategy properly executed.

EVERY STAGE IS CONSIDERED AGAINST FOUR QUESTIONS

Is the planning strategy credible? Is the proposed work practical? Is the programme realistic? Is the commercial outcome clear?
