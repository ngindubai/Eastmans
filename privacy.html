Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED AUGUST 2026

This policy explains how Ēastmans Developments (“Ēastmans”, “we”) handles information in connection with this website.

The short version

This website is an introductory brochure site. It does not currently collect, transmit or store personal information. The enquiry form on the homepage is not connected and cannot be submitted.

Information we collect through this website

None. We do not operate analytics, advertising or tracking services on this site, and we do not set cookies.

The enquiry form is displayed as a preview of a facility that is not yet open. Its submit control is disabled. Anything typed into it remains in your own browser, is not sent anywhere, and is discarded when you close or reload the page.

Server logs

This site is served by our hosting provider, which may record standard technical information such as IP address, request time, requested file, referrer and browser user-agent. These records are generated by the hosting infrastructure for security and reliability purposes and are retained under that provider’s own arrangements.

If you contact us by other means

If you contact us directly, we will use the information you provide solely to respond to and administer that enquiry, and to maintain a record of our correspondence. We do not sell or share enquiry information for marketing purposes.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data we hold about you, including rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection, and the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights in relation to correspondence you have had with us, please use the contact route through which you originally reached us.

When the enquiry form opens

Before the enquiry form is enabled, this policy will be updated to record what is collected, the lawful basis for processing, how long it is retained, and the identity and contact details of the data controller.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website develops. The date above records when it was last revised.
