06 / Private investment

Private capital. Project-specific conversations.

Eastmans develops relationships with private investors interested in selected London property opportunities.

We do not take a one-size-fits-all approach. Each opportunity is considered individually, based on the underlying property, planning strategy, delivery programme, capital requirements, principal risks and intended outcome.

Our investor relationships begin with a private conversation. This allows us to understand an investor’s objectives before discussing whether a particular opportunity may be suitable.

Where there is genuine alignment, Eastmans can introduce appropriate opportunities for private, project-specific consideration.
