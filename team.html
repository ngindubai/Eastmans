The team

The people behind the work.

A hands-on development business led by the people responsible for its acquisitions, planning and delivery.

01 / Co-founders

Founder oversight at every stage.

Adnan and Kashan bring complementary commercial and delivery experience to each project. Together they maintain a clear line of responsibility from the first appraisal to the final handover.

Adnan

Co-founder

Adnan leads acquisitions, commercial appraisal and investment strategy. His focus is on identifying the planning potential within existing buildings, testing each opportunity against realistic market evidence and defining a clear route to value before capital is committed.

  • Acquisitions
  • Commercial appraisal
  • Investment strategy

Kashan

Co-founder

Kashan leads construction delivery, programme and cost control. He coordinates the practical decisions that move a scheme from design into construction, working closely with consultants and contractors to maintain quality, manage risk and keep the commercial objective in view.

  • Construction delivery
  • Programme
  • Cost control

Specialist experience around the founders.

An experienced team supports the day-to-day control of operations, development, construction and planning. Clear ownership of each discipline keeps decisions informed and delivery coordinated.
