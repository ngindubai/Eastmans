01 / The team

A business run by its founders.

Ēastmans is owned and led by the people who appraise its schemes, walk its sites and answer to its investors. Nothing important is delegated beyond their reach, and nothing is committed to that they have not examined themselves.

Hands-on development management London-wide / East London specialists

02 / Founders

Close to the work, close to the numbers.

Adnan and Kashan built Ēastmans around a straightforward working method. Every scheme is appraised by the people who will deliver it, and every commitment is one they expect to stand behind in person.

Between them they cover the full life of a project, from acquisition and planning strategy through design, construction and the eventual sale or letting. Investors deal with them directly at every stage.

Adnan Founder

Shapes the strategy on each scheme and leads its commercial side, from acquisition and appraisal to the planning position that underpins value.

Kashan Founder

Leads delivery. Programme, contractors and cost are managed at close range, with the drawings checked against the building as work proceeds.

03 / On site

Oversight happens on site.

The founders spend their time on site rather than behind a dashboard. Work is checked against the drawings while it can still be changed cheaply, and problems are dealt with while they are small.

+ EXISTING STRUCTURE / RETAINEDSURVEYED BEFORE WORK OPENS UP

Most Ēastmans schemes involve existing buildings, which reward experience and punish assumptions. Knowing what a Victorian terrace will accept, and what it will not, is a large part of how risk is kept out of the programme.

04 / Technical control

Complex work, properly sequenced.

Structural alterations and basement work carry the highest stakes in residential development. Ēastmans takes them on with method. Engineers are appointed early, temporary works are designed before anything is opened up, and the agreed sequence is one the whole team can hold to.

01 AppraisalCost, programme and method are tested before purchase. Schemes that fail the test are left alone.
02 SequenceTemporary works and structural stages are agreed with the engineers before work opens up.
03 ControlProgress is measured against the drawings and the programme, week by week.

05 / The wider team

Known teams, repeat work.

The contractors and consultants around Ēastmans are long-standing relationships rather than lowest bids. Trades are chosen for how they treat existing buildings, and the professional team is coordinated by people who understand the drawings they are issuing.

06 / The result

The building is the evidence.

A finished Ēastmans scheme keeps what gave the property its character and adds what it lacked. That standard is commercial as much as aesthetic. Well-finished period buildings let and sell on their own merits, and they keep doing so.
